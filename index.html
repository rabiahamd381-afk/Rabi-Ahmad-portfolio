FAQs

Questions businesses ask me

Which businesses do you work with?

I work with small and growing businesses that want inquiries and customers from Facebook and Instagram — local services, clinics, institutes, online stores and similar businesses. If your customers are on Facebook or Instagram, a well-planned Meta Ads campaign can reach them.

What do you need to start a campaign?

To start, I need your business goal, your offer, your target location, an idea of your budget, and access to the required business assets — your Facebook Page and ad account. With those in place, I can plan and set up your campaign.

Are the ad budget and your management fee separate?

Yes — completely separate. The ad spend is paid by you directly to Meta for running your ads. My service fee covers planning, setup, management and reporting. You'll always know exactly how much goes to Meta and how much is my fee.

How much time is needed to evaluate results?

It depends on your business, budget and campaign goal. A fair review period is agreed before launch, so the campaign gets enough data for honest decisions. I don't promise fixed sales — I test, measure and improve based on real results.

Do you also provide ad creatives?

Yes — ad copy, headlines and creative direction (the ideas and guidance for your ad visuals) are part of my service. Complete graphic design and video production are a separate scope, which we'll agree on clearly before starting if you need them.

Do you also handle automation and content?

Yes. Along with Meta Ads, I set up Facebook automation for instant replies and follow-ups, and create AI short-form videos for Reels and Shorts, so your ads and your content work together.

How often will I receive reports?

You'll receive regular performance updates in simple, understandable language — what was spent, what results came in and what I'm improving next. The exact reporting schedule is agreed with you at the start of the campaign.
